Marketing team

Ten marketing departments and every step each one takes, tied to the feature that does it. Steps marked every send are done by Smart Brain for each slot it builds; open any slot on /brain and its checks list what each department did for that send. A step that is only partly built says what is missing.

Technical SEO and answer-engine audit

Reads the active brand's own website (the one on its record) and checks each page it reaches: title, meta description, H1, language, canonical, alt text, viewport, Open Graph and schema.org data, plus robots.txt and the sitemap. It also lists the words the site's titles and headings use most. No search volumes: that needs a keyword data source, which is not connected.
