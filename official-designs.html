The store, rebuilt as a 3D scene

A true-to-brand recreation of the store's digital signature rendered as a continuous WebGL scene: floating product panels, glassmorphic surfaces, brand-cloned colours and typography. Live catalog and pricing come from the regional Shopify storefront; historical metrics from the Snowflake to Supabase daily mirror. The scene ships in both variations, dark background with light text, and light background with dark text, and degrades automatically to a fast 2D brand layout on low-end, mobile, reduced-motion or crawler traffic so conversion is never sacrificed.
