Verification

What we can prove

How each claim is backed. Rule for copy: state only what is checkable, and never imply a brand partnership.

Original base

Every design is painted on a 100% original branded pair. Describe it as an original brand product hand-painted by KNICKGASM, and never as a collaboration or endorsement.

Durable finish

Water and scratch resistant, applied and cured in the studio. Claim it plainly and show it being applied; never claim more than the finish delivers.

Design provenance register

Every design is logged as original artwork, fan interpretation, or licence-required. Retire quickly rather than defend, and keep the register regional.

Buyer cohorts

The four audiences

The demographic and behavioural segments every brief targets. Product avatars are the persuasion layer that speaks to these buyers.

Fandom Collectors

Loyalist
Income band
$60k to $140k
Design preference
Anime, gaming, football, cars

Buys the design, not the shoe. Arrives searching a character, a club or a game. Full-margin, protect from a discount habit. Hook: the design nobody else will own.

Grail Collectors

High-intent
Income band
$100k to $220k
Design preference
Air Jordan and Adidas Samba customs

Buys rarity and craft. Compares against resale grails and bespoke commissions. Highest order value in the catalog. Hook: singular beats rare, on a 100% original base.

Occasion & Gift Buyers

Seasonal
Income band
$70k to $180k
Design preference
Wedding, milestone, celebrity, bling

Buys for a person and a date. Least price-sensitive, most deadline-sensitive demand in the range. Hook: a gift that cannot be duplicated, delivered on time.

Curious First-Timers

Switcher
Income band
$45k to $95k
Design preference
Converse and Court Vision customs

Has seen cheaper painted pairs and is weighing the risk. Price-aware and proof-hungry. Hook: the original base, the durable finish, and a stated 10 to 15 day window.
