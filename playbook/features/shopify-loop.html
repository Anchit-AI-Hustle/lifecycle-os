Shopify CDN Asset Ingestion Loop

Open the Shopify content files area, upload the generated asset, then paste the resulting cdn.shopify.com URL back here to register it for the local activation sync.

Pipeline

Two-step ingestion

1. Shopify Content CDN Route

Direct route to the Knickgasm UK store content files. Upload the generated asset there to mint a cdn.shopify.com URL.

Open Shopify content files ↗

admin.shopify.com/store/knickgasmuk/content/files

2. Live Activation Asset Sync

Paste the CDN URL Shopify returns. It is validated for the cdn.shopify.com host, then registered below to trigger the local activation workflow.

    Sync registers assets in this session only. Wire the activation workflow endpoint to persist them to the OS.
