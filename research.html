Tier 1, Global athletic majors

Nike, Adidas, Puma. They supply the BLANK canvas Knickgasm paints on; they are not competitors for the customisation job.

Tier 2, India custom-sneaker studios

Direct competitors: Moreiarty, Sneak Peek Shoes, The Leather Works (TLW Art House), Shoes Your Daddy, Sneakboo, Courtside, MD Customs.

Tier 3, Sneaker retail and marketplaces

Compete for the same sneakerhead wallet: VegNonVeg, Superkicks, CrepDog Crew, Hype Fly, FindYourKicks, Mainstreet Marketplace.

Tier 4, Indie design-led Indian brands

Comet, Gully Labs, Thaely, Banjaaran Studio. Design-forward originals rather than customisation.

Tier 5, Global customisation benchmarks

The Shoe Surgeon, Kickstradomis, and the official programmes Nike By You and Converse By You.

Strategic read
  • Customisation is a service margin on someone else's silhouette: the moat is artist quality and turnaround, not tooling or distribution.
  • Fandom collections (anime, football, cars, gaming) are the demand engine; core AF1 blanks are the volume base.
  • Every claim must state the pairs are hand-painted on 100% original sneakers, never replicas.
Known data gaps (not estimated)
  • Category-level customisation spend in India is not separately published; only the broader sneaker market is sized.
  • No public share-of-market figure exists for any Indian customisation studio.

Measured in Data Analysis

This page studies the market around the brand: sizing, competitive tiers and the strategic read, each figure carrying its published source. Revenue, orders, average order value, cohorts, paid media and owned channels are measured from the brand's own connected data on one surface, per market, so no two pages can report a different number for the same thing.

Intraday pacing and revenue projection are not shown anywhere until an hourly order stream is connected. Until then there is nothing to pace against, and a projection built on a placeholder run-rate reads as a measurement.
