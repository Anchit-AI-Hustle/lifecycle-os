Lifecycle OS

One platform, one brand at a time

A lifecycle-marketing operating system: analytics and cohorts, a rolling campaign calendar, generation of mailers, ads and landing pages, competitor benchmarking, and a brand assistant that can operate the whole stack.

How brands work

You onboard a brand once: identity, colour schema, typography, voice, and where its catalogue comes from. Those values are not decoration. They become the design tokens the entire suite renders through, and the rules every generation prompt obeys, so the app re-skins to your brand and the assets it produces stay on-brand by construction rather than by review.

You can keep more than one brand and switch between them. Each is a separate workspace with its own catalogue, cohorts, campaigns and market study.

Why features are locked until a brand is active

Almost every screen here is brand-specific: the products, the competitive set, the market sizing, the tone of a generated email. Rendering one brand's data inside another brand's workspace would be misleading, and inventing placeholder data would be worse. So until a brand is selected or created, the features stay closed and only this page and the brand setup remain open.

What it will not do

  • Invent a product, price, URL, review, rating or statistic. Missing data is reported as [DATA REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH] rather than filled in.
  • Carry one brand's facts into another brand's assets.
  • Ship a palette that fails WCAG AA contrast, or a dark-neutral page surface.
