Lifecycle OS

One platform, one brand at a time

A lifecycle-marketing operating system: analytics and cohorts, a rolling campaign calendar, generation of mailers, ads and landing pages, competitor benchmarking, and a brand assistant that can operate the whole stack.

How brands work

You onboard a brand once: identity, colour schema, typography, voice, and where its catalogue comes from. Those values are not decoration. They become the design tokens the entire suite renders through, and the rules every generation prompt obeys, so the app re-skins to your brand and the assets it produces stay on-brand by construction rather than by review.

You can keep more than one brand and switch between them. Each is a separate workspace with its own catalogue, cohorts, campaigns and market study.

Every feature starts from your brand

Almost every screen here is brand-specific: the products, the competitive set, the market sizing, the tone of a generated email. That is why you choose or create a brand first. Once it is active, every feature works from that brand’s own data, and until then this page and the brand setup are ready for you.

Rules and standards it follows

  • Every fact is sourced. Products, prices, links, reviews, ratings and figures come only from your brand’s own record. Anything still missing is marked [DATA REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH] so you can add it before launch.
  • Each brand has its own workspace. One brand’s facts appear only in that brand’s assets.
  • Every design is readable. Colours meet WCAG AA contrast and pages sit on a light surface.
  • Every mailer and ad is quality-checked. The logo linked to the brand’s website, an unsubscribe line, short copy, hosted images and an honest subject line, checked before anything is sent.
