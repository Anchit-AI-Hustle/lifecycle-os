Create a track

Make a new track for your brand

Music for a product, a sale, a launch or the brand itself. The brief is written from your brand's own record (its name, voice, tagline and products) and you can change anything. It is composed by Lyria, Google's music model, when your workspace or this deployment has a Gemini key that can use it.

Generate here costs credits. It tries every music model a key reaches: Lyria (Gemini), ElevenLabs Music and Replicate (ACE-Step sings your lyrics, MusicGen plays instrumentals). Your own keys, connected on AI Models and Platform Connections, are used first and are billed to your own accounts instead of your credits. Only when no model answers does it make a short instrumental sketch, and it says so. Suno has no public API, so Open in Suno copies the brief and opens Suno's Create page: paste it into the Style box there.
