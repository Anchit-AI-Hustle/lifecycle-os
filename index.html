Why accuracy matters

Lifecycle marketing runs on volume. Mailers, ads and landing pages, one per cohort per market per week, and each one has to be on-brand and correct.

Speed and accuracy, together. Generic AI tools are quick, but they can fill a gap with a price, a rating or a review nobody approved, and with several brands in one place they can mix up whose facts are whose. Here, every asset is built only from your brand’s own record.

Your brand stays protected. Accurate claims keep every send within advertising standards, and every asset carries your own products, colours and words.

How it is built: a fact the platform does not have is marked for you to fill in, and each brand’s data stays in its own workspace. This is enforced in the code and confirmed by automated tests, so it does not depend on anyone remembering a checklist.

How a brand works here

  1. Describe the brand. Identity, colour schema, typography, voice, and where its catalogue comes from. Start from a profile or enter your own.
  2. It becomes the system. Those values are compiled into design tokens and prompt rules, so the app re-skins and generated assets are on-brand by construction, not by review.
  3. Connect the catalogue. Products, or events, programmes, sections and plans. A publisher and a product brand are modelled differently on purpose.
  4. Work. Plan, generate, review, ship. Switch brands and everything switches with you.

Rules and standards it follows

Every fact is sourced. Products, prices, links, reviews, ratings and figures come only from your brand’s own record. Anything still missing is clearly marked [DATA REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH] so you can add it before launch.

Each brand has its own workspace. Your catalogue, competitors, analytics and market study belong to your brand alone. Where a brand has no data yet, the page shows what to connect next.

Every design is readable. Colours meet WCAG AA contrast and pages sit on a light surface. Each palette is checked before a brand goes live.

Every mailer and ad is quality-checked. The brand’s logo linked to its website, an unsubscribe line, short readable copy, properly hosted images and an honest subject line are all checked before anything is sent.
