Privacy Policy

Lifecycle OS. Last updated: 13 August 2026.

Lifecycle OS ("the App") is a retention and lifecycle-marketing workspace. This workspace is operated by the workspace owner and is used to analyse that organisation's own customer data, plan marketing campaigns, and generate email, ad and landing-page content. It is not a consumer product; access is restricted to people that organisation has authorised.

Each brand workspace is private to its owner. Data and content created in one workspace are not visible to any other workspace.

Google user data we access

When you sign in with Google, the App requests only your basic profile: your name, email address, and profile picture (the openid, email, and profile scopes). We do not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Google Ads, or any other Google service data through Google sign-in.

How we use it

Your Google profile is used solely to authenticate you and to display your name and picture within the App. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than authentication and access control.

Storage and retention

Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. A minimal session record (your user id and email) is stored to keep you signed in and to authorise access. Application data you create (campaign plans, generated content) is stored in our Supabase database and, for convenience, your browser's local storage. You can request deletion of your account record at any time (see Contact).

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your Google user data or personal information with third parties. Data is processed only by the App and its infrastructure providers (e.g. Supabase for authentication and storage, Vercel for hosting) acting on our behalf.

Limited Use disclosure

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only use Google user data to provide and improve the sign-in and access features described above, and we do not transfer it except as necessary to provide those features, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition.

Third-party marketing platforms

Where a team connects the App to marketing platforms (for example ad or email platforms), the App reads reporting data in read-only mode to display analytics. That data belongs to the connecting organisation's own business accounts and is not obtained through your Google sign-in.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by the App at any time via the contact below.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the workspace owner.
