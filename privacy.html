Privacy Policy

Lifecycle OS. Effective 10 October 2026. Operated by Anchit's AI Hustle.

Lifecycle OS ("the App") is a retention and lifecycle-marketing workspace. This workspace is operated by the workspace owner and is used to analyse that organisation's own customer data, plan marketing campaigns, and generate email, ad and landing-page content. It is not a consumer product; access is restricted to people that organisation has authorised.

Each brand workspace is private to its owner. Data and content created in one workspace are not visible to any other workspace.

Signing in: what we collect

Sign-in is with a Google account, through Supabase Auth. There is no other sign-in. When you choose Sign in with Google, Google asks you to pick an account and, with your consent, gives the App your basic profile only: your name, your verified email address and your profile picture. The App does not ask Google for anything else, does not read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or contacts, and never sees your Google password. Supabase Auth keeps your account record (your Google account's id, name, email and picture) and issues the session that keeps you signed in.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time from Google Account permissions.

Other data the App keeps

  • Workspace and brand data you enter or import: product catalogues, cohort definitions, marketing calendars, brand asset links and campaign performance figures.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type and system event logs, used for security and performance monitoring.

How we use it

Your Google profile is used solely to identify your account and keep you signed in. Your name and picture are shown to you within the App. We do not use them for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than authentication and access control. Workspace data is used to plan, schedule and generate that brand's own campaigns (emails, ads and landing pages) and is kept isolated per brand. We do not sell your data, do not share it with advertising platforms, and do not use your private data to train generalised AI or ML models without your explicit consent.

Storage and retention

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest. Workspaces are isolated so one brand's data is never accessible to another.

Your sign-in session (a Supabase session token) lives in this browser and is ended when you sign out. Brand workspaces you create are saved to your account, private to it, or on this device when you are not signed in or the account database cannot be reached.

Application data you create (campaign plans, generated content, knowledge-base entries) is stored in the App's Supabase database where the deployment has one, and, for convenience, in your browser's local storage. You can request deletion of your account record at any time (see Contact).

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your name, email address, Google profile or any personal information with third parties, and we do not share Google user data with advertising platforms. Data is processed only by the App and its infrastructure providers (Supabase for the account and application data, Vercel for hosting) acting on our behalf.

Third-party marketing platforms

Where a team connects the App to marketing platforms (for example ad or email platforms), the App reads reporting data in read-only mode to display analytics. That data belongs to the connecting organisation's own business accounts and is not obtained through your sign-in.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by the App at any time via the contact below.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the workspace owner.

The App itself is developed and operated by Anchit Tandon (Anchit's AI Hustle), anchit.tandon@gmail.com, lifecycle-os.anchit-tandon.com. To permanently delete your account and all associated workspace data, email that address with the subject "Data Deletion Request".

Historical: sign-in with a mobile number and a PIN (28 September 2026 to 10 October 2026)

For a period sign-in used a mobile number and a 4-digit PIN. It is switched off: the App no longer accepts a number or a PIN, and a mobile-number sign-in left in a browser is ended the next time the App opens there (the brands it kept on that device are kept). Accounts of that kind held the mobile number with its country code, the name typed at sign-up and a salted hash of the PIN, never the PIN itself, in the account database or in that browser's local storage. You can ask for any such record to be deleted (see Contact).

Historical: sign-in with a Google account (5 October 2026 to 9 October 2026)

Google sign-in was also offered for a few days before the mobile-number sign-in replaced it, and is the sign-in again since 10 October 2026, as described above.
