Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 6, 2026  |  Application: LifeCycle OS

LifeCycle OS, operated by Anchit's AI Hustle, provides autonomous lifecycle marketing and retention workflow automation for retail and e-commerce brands. This Privacy Policy details our data collection, usage, and protection practices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Sign-in details: Sign-in is with a mobile number and a 4-digit PIN. We keep the mobile number (with its country code), the name you type when you sign up, and a salted hash of your PIN, never the PIN itself. The number is not verified by SMS. A sign-in lasts 90 days. Where a deployment has no account database, the account is kept in your browser's local storage instead, on that device only.
  • Workspace & Brand Data: Product catalogs, customer lifecycle cohort definitions, marketing calendar schedules, brand asset links, and campaign performance metrics.
  • Device & Log Data: IP address, browser type, and system event logs for security and performance monitoring.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your session and protect workspace access.
  • To plan, schedule, and generate brand-specific marketing campaigns (emails, ads, and landing pages).
  • To prevent cross-brand data leakage and isolate brand catalogs.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

LifeCycle OS's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do not sell Google user data to third parties.
  • We do not share your Google data with external advertising platforms.
  • We do not use your private data to train generalized AI/ML models without explicit user consent.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS (HTTPS) and encrypted at rest. Workspaces are strictly isolated to ensure one brand's data is never accessible to another tenant.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your information only as long as necessary to provide services. You can revoke LifeCycle OS's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account Permissions. To permanently delete your account and all associated workspace data, email anchit.tandon@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request".
