Privacy Policy

Lifecycle OS. Effective 6 October 2026. Operated by Anchit's AI Hustle.

Lifecycle OS ("the App") is a retention and lifecycle-marketing workspace. This workspace is operated by the workspace owner and is used to analyse that organisation's own customer data, plan marketing campaigns, and generate email, ad and landing-page content. It is not a consumer product; access is restricted to people that organisation has authorised.

Each brand workspace is private to its owner. Data and content created in one workspace are not visible to any other workspace.

Signing in: what we collect

Sign-in is with a mobile number and a 4-digit PIN. There is no other sign-in. The App keeps the mobile number (with its country code), the name you type when you sign up, and a salted hash of your PIN, never the PIN itself. In the account database the PIN is hashed with scrypt and a per-account salt; in a Supabase project it is first turned into a long password with a secret only the App's server holds, and Supabase keeps only a bcrypt hash of that. The number is not verified by SMS. A sign-in lasts 90 days. Where a deployment has no account database, the account is kept in your browser's local storage instead (PBKDF2-SHA256 with a random salt), on that device only and under a key private to that account.

Other data the App keeps

  • Workspace and brand data you enter or import: product catalogues, cohort definitions, marketing calendars, brand asset links and campaign performance figures.
  • Device and log data: IP address, browser type and system event logs, used for security and performance monitoring.

How we use it

These details are used solely to identify your account and keep you signed in. Your name is shown to you within the App. We do not use them for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than authentication and access control. Workspace data is used to plan, schedule and generate that brand's own campaigns (emails, ads and landing pages) and is kept isolated per brand. We do not sell your data, do not share it with advertising platforms, and do not use your private data to train generalised AI or ML models without your explicit consent.

Storage and retention

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest. Workspaces are isolated so one brand's data is never accessible to another.

Your sign-in session lives in this browser and is ended when you sign out. Brand workspaces you create are saved to your account, private to it, or on this device when the deployment has no account database.

Application data you create (campaign plans, generated content, knowledge-base entries) is stored in the App's Supabase database where the deployment has one, and, for convenience, in your browser's local storage. You can request deletion of your account record at any time (see Contact).

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your name, mobile number or any personal information with third parties. Data is processed only by the App and its infrastructure providers (Supabase or Neon for the account and application data, Vercel for hosting) acting on our behalf.

Third-party marketing platforms

Where a team connects the App to marketing platforms (for example ad or email platforms), the App reads reporting data in read-only mode to display analytics. That data belongs to the connecting organisation's own business accounts and is not obtained through your sign-in.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by the App at any time via the contact below.

Contact
