Privacy Policy

Lifecycle OS. Last updated: 29 September 2026.

Lifecycle OS ("the App") is a retention and lifecycle-marketing workspace. This workspace is operated by the workspace owner and is used to analyse that organisation's own customer data, plan marketing campaigns, and generate email, ad and landing-page content. It is not a consumer product; access is restricted to people that organisation has authorised.

Each brand workspace is private to its owner. Data and content created in one workspace are not visible to any other workspace.

Signing in: what we collect

Sign-in is by mobile number and a 4-digit PIN. There is no other sign-in. When you create an account or sign in, the App keeps:

  • your mobile number (with its country code) and the name you type;
  • a salted hash of your PIN, never the PIN itself. In the database the hash is scrypt with a per-account salt; in your browser it is PBKDF2-SHA256 with a random salt. Neither can be turned back into the PIN, and nothing in the App can show you a PIN, only let you set a new one;
  • the sign-in sessions you create: a random token kept in your browser, of which the database stores only a SHA-256 digest, the time it expires, and an optional label for the device;
  • a count of wrong PIN attempts on the account and, after five, the time until which it is locked;
  • the network address a sign-in attempt came from, kept for ten minutes to limit how many attempts one address can make.

Your number is not verified by SMS and is used only to identify your account. The App does not ask for, receive or store an email address, a profile picture or any social or third-party account for sign-in.

How we use it

These details are used solely to identify your account, keep you signed in, and protect the account from PIN guessing (the attempt counter, the lock and the per-address limit). The name you typed is shown to you within the App. We do not use any of it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than authentication and access control.

Storage and retention

Where your account lives depends on how the deployment is configured, and the App tells you which applies in a sentence under the sign-in form and under your name once signed in:

  • In the database. When the deployment has an account database configured (a Neon PostgreSQL database), your account, its PIN hash and your sessions are stored there, in the app_users, app_sessions and app_rate_limits tables. Sessions expire after 90 days, or sooner when you sign out (that session) or sign out everywhere (all of them). Expired sessions are deleted; attempt-limit records expire after ten minutes.
  • On this device only. When no database is configured, or it is not answering, your account and PIN hash are stored in your browser's local storage on that device and never reach a server. Clearing the browser's site data removes them; the App cannot recover a device-only account.

Application data you create (campaign plans, generated content, knowledge-base entries) is stored in the App's Supabase database where the deployment has one, and, for convenience, in your browser's local storage. Brand workspaces created by a mobile-number account are kept in your browser's local storage on that device, under a key private to that account: another person signing in on the same browser cannot see them, and signing out does not delete them. You can request deletion of your account record at any time (see Contact).

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or any personal information with third parties. Data is processed only by the App and its infrastructure providers (Neon for the account database, Supabase for application data, Vercel for hosting) acting on our behalf.

Third-party marketing platforms

Where a team connects the App to marketing platforms (for example ad or email platforms), the App reads reporting data in read-only mode to display analytics. That data belongs to the connecting organisation's own business accounts and is not obtained through your sign-in.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by the App at any time via the contact below.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the workspace owner.

Historical: sign-in with Google (until 28 September 2026)

Sign-in with Google was withdrawn on 28 September 2026 and replaced by the mobile number and PIN described above. The App no longer requests, receives or stores Google user data, and the Google API scopes below are no longer requested. The text that follows described that sign-in and is kept for the record only.

When you signed in with Google, the App requested only your basic profile, your name, email address and profile picture, under the openid, email and profile scopes, and never Gmail, Google Drive, Google Ads or any other Google service data. That profile was used solely to authenticate you and to display your name and picture within the App; a minimal session record (user id and email) was held by Supabase Auth to keep you signed in. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adhered to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: Google user data was used only to provide the sign-in and access features, and was not transferred except as necessary to provide them, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition.
