Privacy Policy

Lifecycle OS. Last updated: 5 October 2026.

Lifecycle OS ("the App") is a retention and lifecycle-marketing workspace. This workspace is operated by the workspace owner and is used to analyse that organisation's own customer data, plan marketing campaigns, and generate email, ad and landing-page content. It is not a consumer product; access is restricted to people that organisation has authorised.

Each brand workspace is private to its owner. Data and content created in one workspace are not visible to any other workspace.

Signing in: what we collect

Sign-in is with Google. There is no other sign-in. When you sign in, the App requests only your basic profile: your name, email address and profile picture, under the openid, email and profile scopes. It does not request Gmail, Google Drive, Google Ads or any other Google service.

  • your name, email address and profile picture, used to show who is signed in;
  • the session Supabase Auth keeps so you stay signed in: an access token in this browser, renewed by Supabase, and ended when you sign out.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide sign-in and access, and is not transferred except as necessary to provide them, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition.

How we use it

These details are used solely to identify your account and keep you signed in. Your name and picture are shown to you within the App. We do not use them for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than authentication and access control.

Storage and retention

Your Google sign-in is a user in the deployment's Supabase project. The session lives in this browser and is revoked in Supabase when you sign out. Brand workspaces you create are saved to that account, private to it.

Application data you create (campaign plans, generated content, knowledge-base entries) is stored in the App's Supabase database where the deployment has one, and, for convenience, in your browser's local storage. You can request deletion of your account record at any time (see Contact).

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your name, email address or any personal information with third parties. Data is processed only by the App and its infrastructure providers (Supabase for the account and application data, Google for sign-in, Vercel for hosting) acting on our behalf.

Third-party marketing platforms

Where a team connects the App to marketing platforms (for example ad or email platforms), the App reads reporting data in read-only mode to display analytics. That data belongs to the connecting organisation's own business accounts and is not obtained through your sign-in.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data held by the App at any time via the contact below.

Contact

For privacy questions or data-deletion requests, contact the workspace owner.

Historical: sign-in with a mobile number (28 September 2026 to 5 October 2026)

Sign-in with a mobile number and a 4-digit PIN was withdrawn on 5 October 2026 and replaced by Google, described above. The App no longer asks for a mobile number or a PIN. The text that follows described that sign-in and is kept for the record only.

While it was offered, the App kept the mobile number (with its country code), the name typed at sign-up, and a salted hash of the PIN, never the PIN itself. In the Neon database the hash was scrypt with a per-account salt; in the browser it was PBKDF2-SHA256 with a random salt; in a Supabase project the PIN was first turned into a long password with a secret only the App's server held, and Supabase kept only a bcrypt hash of that password. The number was not verified by SMS. Sessions lasted 90 days. Where no database was configured, the account was stored in the browser's local storage, under a key private to that account.
