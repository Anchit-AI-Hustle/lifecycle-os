MODULE 01
The physics of every ad platform

Three ideas run everything: the auction, the funnel, the metrics.

Google, Meta and TikTok feel different because their surfaces differ. Underneath, they share the same engine. Master these three and you can walk into any ad account and know what you're looking at.

1 · The auction, how the slot gets sold

You never buy a fixed price. You enter a real-time auction that runs the instant a page loads or a feed scrolls. But bid alone doesn't win, platforms protect the user experience, so a relevant, high-quality ad can beat a higher bid. That's the single most important idea in paid media.

The winning formula

Ad Rank = Bid × Quality

On Google it's Bid × Quality Score (+ ad assets). On Meta & TikTok it's Bid × Estimated Action Rate × Ad Quality. Quality is how likely the user is to click / convert / not be annoyed. A great ad literally lowers your cost.

What you actually pay

Second-price logic

You usually pay just enough to beat the advertiser ranked below you, not your full bid. So bidding high isn't "wasting money"; it sets your ceiling, not your price. Improve quality and you can win the same slot for less.

Operator takeaway

You don't optimize "the bid." You optimize quality × relevance × creative, because that's the multiplier that moves your cost without spending a rupee more. Bad creative is an invisible tax on every click.

2 · The funnel, strangers don't buy on sight

Almost nobody buys the first time they meet you. The funnel is just a map of temperature: cold strangers at the top, warm considerers in the middle, hot ready-to-buy at the bottom. Each stage needs a different objective, audience, creative, and metric. Judging a top-funnel ad by bottom-funnel ROAS is the most common way to kill a good campaign.

TOFU · Top

Awareness

Job: get discovered by cold strangers.
Audience: broad / interest / lookalike.
Creative: scroll-stopping hooks, story.
Watch: CPM, reach, hook rate, 3-sec views.

MOFU · Middle

Consideration

Job: earn trust, get engagement.
Audience: engagers, video viewers, visitors.
Creative: proof, reviews, demo, education.
Watch: CTR, landing views, add-to-cart.

BOFU · Bottom

Conversion

Job: close the ready-to-buy.
Audience: cart abandoners, high-intent, brand search.
Creative: offer, urgency, guarantee.
Watch: ROAS, CPA, conversion rate.

3 · The metrics, the language of the machine

Every dashboard is the same eight or nine numbers rearranged. Here's the decoder. Read left to right and the whole funnel is one chain of division.

CPMcost per mille
What it costs to show your ad 1,000 times. The price of attention. (Spend ÷ Impressions) × 1000
CTRclick-thru rate
Of the people who saw it, the % who clicked. Your creative & targeting report card. Clicks ÷ Impressions
CPCcost per click
What you pay for one visit. Falls when CTR & quality rise. Spend ÷ Clicks
CVRconversion rate
Of clickers, the % who bought / signed up. This is your landing page & offer, not the ad. Conversions ÷ Clicks
CPAcost per acquisition
What one customer costs you. Also called CAC. Spend ÷ Conversions
AOVavg order value
Average rupees per order. Raise this and every other number gets easier. Revenue ÷ Orders
ROASreturn on ad spend
Revenue for every ₹1 spent. The headline number. Revenue ÷ Ad Spend
MERmarketing efficiency ratio
Blended ROAS across all channels = total revenue ÷ total spend. The truth number when platforms over-claim credit. Total Revenue ÷ Total Spend
The number that saves accounts · Break-even ROAS

ROAS of 3 sounds great, until your margin is 25%. Your break-even ROAS = 1 ÷ contribution margin. At 40% margin you break even at 2.5×; anything above is profit. Never chase a ROAS target without knowing your break-even first. Try it in the calculator below.

MODULE 02
The intent engine
Google Ads // captures demand that already exists

Google catches people at the exact moment they're looking.

Meta and TikTok create demand by interrupting. Google drops demand that's already there, someone typed "best coffee collection uk" and told you precisely what they want. That intent is why Google converts higher and costs more per click. You're not convincing anyone to want it; you're making sure you're the one they find.

The surfaces, one account, many networks

🔎 Search🛒 Shopping📺 YouTube 🖼 Display (GDN)✨ Demand Gen🤖 Performance Max

Campaign types & when to reach for each

Search
Text ads on results pages. Highest intent. Your bread-and-butter for demand capture: brand defense, non-brand keywords, competitor terms.
Shopping
Product image + price pulled from a Merchant Center feed. Essential for ecommerce, it's the visual grid at the top of product searches.
Performance Max
Goal-in, AI runs every network at once from one asset group + audience signals. Powerful but a black box, feed it great assets and clean conversion data.
Demand Gen
Visual, social-style ads across YouTube, Discover & Gmail. Google's answer to Meta/TikTok for top-funnel discovery. (Replaced Discovery campaigns.)
YouTube / Video
Skippable, non-skippable, bumper, in-feed. Awareness & consideration at scale with sight-sound-motion.
Display
Banners across 2M+ sites. Cheap reach & retargeting; low intent, so guard against junk placements with exclusions.

Account structure, the nesting doll

ACCOUNTbilling, conversion tracking, everything lives here
CAMPAIGNone budget · network · geography · bid strategy · goal
AD GROUPone tight theme of keywords + the ads that match them
KEYWORDS + ADSthe actual search terms you bid on and the copy people see

Keywords & match types, the intent dial

Match type controls how loosely Google interprets a keyword. Loosen for reach, tighten for control.

Broad match
coffee collection → any related search Google guesses. Max reach, needs heavy negatives + smart bidding to stay clean.
Phrase match
"coffee collection" → searches containing that meaning. The balanced default.
Exact match
[coffee collection] → that intent only. Tightest control, highest intent, least volume.
Negative keywords
The unsung hero. Block "free", "jobs", "recipe" etc. Mine the search-terms report weekly or bleed budget on junk.

Smart Bidding, hand the algorithm a goal, not a bid

Maximize Conversions / tCPA
Get the most conversions, or hit a target cost per conversion. Lead-gen default.
Max Conversion Value / tROAS
Optimize for revenue, or hit a target return. Ecommerce default, but it needs value data flowing in.

MODULE 03
The demand-generation engine
Meta Ads // Facebook + Instagram · creates demand with creative

Meta finds buyers who weren't looking, using the creative as the targeting.

On Meta, nobody asked to see your ad. You interrupt a scroll and earn the next half-second. For a decade the game was surgical audience targeting; today the algorithm is so good at finding buyers that the winning play is broad targeting + relentless creative testing. Your creative now does the targeting, the hook decides who stops.

Structure, the three-layer stack

CAMPAIGNthe objective + budget (Advantage+ / CBO shares budget across ad sets automatically)
AD SETwho (audience), where (placements), the optimization event, budget if ABO
ADthe creative, image, video, carousel. Where campaigns are actually won or lost.

Objectives (ODAX), pick the outcome, the algorithm optimizes for it

AwarenessTrafficEngagement LeadsApp PromotionSales ★

Whatever you choose, Meta shows your ad to people likely to take that action. Choose "Traffic" and you'll get cheap clickers who never buy. Choose "Sales" and optimize for purchase, always optimize for the action closest to money that you have enough volume to feed.

Targeting, from surgical to broad

Core audiences
Age, location, interests, behaviors. Still useful, but increasingly the algorithm beats manual picks.
Custom audiences
Your own data: site visitors (via Pixel/CAPI), customer lists, video viewers, page engagers. The retargeting fuel.
Lookalikes
"Find more people like my buyers." Seed it with your best customers, not all traffic.
Advantage+ (broad)
Hand Meta minimal constraints and let AI find buyers. The modern default for prospecting when creative volume is high.

The Learning Phase, the rule that trips everyone

A new ad set is "learning" until it gets roughly 50 optimization events (e.g. purchases) in a week. Until it exits, performance is unstable. So: don't split budget into tiny fragmented ad sets, and don't edit a live ad set constantly, every meaningful change resets learning. Patience is a performance setting.

Signal, Pixel + Conversions API

After iOS 14 / ATT wrecked browser tracking, you need the Pixel (browser) + Conversions API (server) feeding events back to Meta. Weak signal = the algorithm optimizes blind = wasted spend. This is plumbing, but it's the plumbing everything else depends on.

The 2026 truth

Targeting is 80% solved by the algorithm; creative is the lever you still control. Winning Meta accounts test many creatives per week, hooks, angles, formats, UGC vs. polished, and let the auction pick. Treat creative like a portfolio of bets, not a monthly deliverable.

MODULE 04
The entertainment engine
TikTok Ads // entertainment-first · sound-on · native or die

On TikTok, the ad that looks like an ad loses. Make a TikTok, not a commercial.

TikTok's own motto, "Don't make ads, make TikToks", is the whole strategy. The feed is entertainment, sound-on, native. A polished 16:9 brand film gets scrolled in half a second; a raw, hook-first, phone-shot clip that feels like organic content wins. And because content decays fast here, TikTok is a game of creative velocity, volume and freshness beat perfection.

Structure, mirrors Meta

CAMPAIGNobjective + budget
AD GROUPtargeting, placement, budget, bid, optimization goal
ADthe video, usually 9:16, sound-on, hook in the first 3 seconds

Ad formats, Spark Ads are the cheat code

In-Feed Ads
Native videos in the For You feed. The workhorse format.
Spark Ads ★
Boost a real organic post (yours or a creator's) as an ad. Keeps likes/comments/social proof → consistently the top performer. Start here.
TopView / Takeover
Premium first-thing-you-see placements. Big reach, big budgets, awareness plays.
Shop / Collection
Shoppable formats + TikTok Shop for in-app purchase. Closing the loop from entertainment to checkout.

Targeting & automation

Interests & behaviors
Based on what people watch, like, and engage with, richer than stated interests.
Custom & lookalike
Same idea as Meta, upload data, retarget engagers, expand from best customers.
Smart Performance Campaign
TikTok's fully-automated PMax-equivalent: give assets + goal, it handles targeting & bidding.
Creative-led expansion
Keep targeting broad and let the video decide who it resonates with. The hook is your real targeting layer.
The 3-second law

You have ~3 seconds before the thumb moves. Lead with the hook, the payoff, the problem, the pattern-break, never a slow logo intro. Use trending sounds, native captions, real faces. Creative fatigue hits faster than Meta, so refresh constantly. On TikTok, the media buyer's job is 20% buying and 80% keeping the creative pipeline full.

You don't pick a platform. You build a system where they feed each other.

The real journey isn't linear: someone sees a TikTok, gets retargeted on Instagram, then Googles your brand and buys. Each platform played a role. The mistake is judging them in isolation. Here's how operators actually allocate.

How to split the budget

Rule of thumb · 70 / 20 / 10

Protect the proven, fund the future

70% into what already works (usually demand capture + your best channel). 20% into scaling promising bets. 10% into pure experiments, new platforms, new creative angles, new audiences. This keeps you profitable today without going blind tomorrow.

Where to start with limited budget

Start where the intent is

Small budget? Begin at the bottom of the funnel, brand search + retargeting, because it converts cheapest and proves the machine works. Then expand up-funnel into Meta/TikTok prospecting once you can afford to warm cold audiences. Mind the floors: Meta ≈ ₹1.5k+/day per ad set, TikTok ≈ ₹1.5k+/day/campaign, Google Search ₹800+/day for real signal.

MODULE 06
The part most people get wrong

If you add up every platform's ROAS, you get a number bigger than reality.

Every platform claims credit for the same sale. Meta says it drove it, Google says it drove it, TikTok says it drove it, so in-platform ROAS is always inflated. Growing spend on "reported" numbers is how brands quietly go broke. Here's the measurement stack that keeps you honest.

Blended ROAS / MER
Total revenue ÷ total ad spend, across everything. Can't be gamed by attribution. Watch this trend more than any single platform's number.
Incrementality tests
Turn a channel off in some geos (holdout) and measure the real lift. Answers the only question that matters: "would this sale have happened anyway?"
Post-purchase survey
"How did you hear about us?" at checkout. Low-tech, attribution-proof, and often the truest read on top-of-funnel platforms like TikTok.
Attribution windows
Know what you're counting: a 7-day-click / 1-day-view purchase is a very different claim than a view-through. Compare like with like across platforms.
MMM (media mix modeling)
Statistical model of how each channel drives sales over time. For bigger budgets, the privacy-proof successor to cookie tracking.
First-party data + CAPI
Server-side event tracking is now mandatory infrastructure. Better signal in → better optimization out → lower costs. Not optional.
The testing loop · how growth actually compounds
Hypothesis"UGC hook beats studio hook"
Testone variable, enough budget & time to reach significance
Read the result, did it beat control?
Winner
Scale it & make it the new control
Loser
Kill it, keep the learning

Prioritize what to test with ICE, Impact × Confidence × Ease. And remember the hierarchy of leverage: offer & product > creative > targeting > bid & budget. Most people obsess over the last two and ignore the first two, where the real money is.
