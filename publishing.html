Read from a platform

Reads need LIVE_CONNECTORS and a connected account, nothing more: reading is not publishing. Metrics are kept so a creative can be compared with this brand’s own history.

Underperforming creatives

A creative is flagged when it sits below this brand’s own median for its platform (three or more creatives are needed for a median), or below a threshold you set. A flag OFFERS a regeneration at its credit price; nothing is regenerated, spent or published until an editor approves it.

Your thresholds (optional)

No platform publishes a universal “good” CTR or ROAS, so none is assumed here. Leave a field empty to rely on the brand’s own median alone. A platform reports CTR in its own unit (Meta as a percentage, Google as a ratio), and a flag only ever compares a creative with others on the same platform.

Drafts awaiting live approval

Paused ads, draft campaigns and private videos this brand created. Approving one queues its own job, stamped with your account, behind the same three switches. A Google Ads ad is created paused and turned on in Google Ads itself: the call that would enable it was not re-read from Google’s documentation here, so it is not offered.

Inbox

Webhook deliveries whose signature was verified and whose account this brand connected: comments, mentions and publish status. A delivery for an account nobody connected is kept unrouted, never guessed onto a brand.
