## 3. Research learnings applied

**From the inspiration library:** gift-locked-to-subscription (AG1), 3-for-2 repeating subscription (Space Goods), low-pressure "Hello, anyone there?" win-back (Space Goods/Grüns), welcome/unboxing education (AG1), occasion-triggered bundles (RYZE), narrative launch emails (Kickstradomis). Flagged: these brands lean on banned-style urgency (LAST CHANCE / FINAL HOURS / while supplies last) — mechanics were adopted, copy re-voiced to KNICKGASM's warm register.

**From the knowledge base:** Dimond framework-first copy (PAS/AIDA/value-stack) and campaign-angle diversity; Kim's hammer-vs-nurture (prospects = Cohort A can take directness; customers = Cohort B get nurture only) and click-focused CTAs; Sanocki's win-back ladder (former buyers are the best audience; never open with a discount).

**Non-engager subject discipline enforced:** under ~40 chars (V2 set runs 23–36), human/curiosity-led, no ALL-CAPS, no exclamation marks, no spam-trigger words, preheader continues (never repeats) the subject, V1 vs V2 test different psychological angles.
