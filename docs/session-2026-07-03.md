## 1. The brief (as given)

Build an automated system that generates a **monthly calendar of emails** for two cohorts:

| Cohort | Definition | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Non-Buyers / Non-Engagers** | On the list, never purchased, not opening/clicking | Open → click → first purchase |
| **B — T&B Buyers / Non-Engagers** | Bought Sneakers & Botanicals before, gone quiet | Reactivate → replenish → cross-grade to Coffee/Supplements |

**Product types & purchase rules:**
| Type | Rule |
|---|---|
| Sneakers & Botanicals (T&B) | **One-time purchase only** — never subscription-framed |
| Coffee Collection | One-time OR subscription — **subscription is the priority push** (LTV) |
| Supplements | Just launched, zero buyers — **subscription priority**; pricing will mirror the coffee model (numbers TBD) |

**Coffee Collection pricing (GBP, confirmed):**
| Pack | One-time | Subscription |
|---|---|---|
| Pack of 1 | £49.99 | £29.99 |
| Pack of 3 | £99.99 | £59.99 (B2G1: £59.99 = 2 × £29.99 → third pack free) |

- **7 free gifts with EVERY order**: Electric Frother, Recipe Booklet, Plantable Paper, Colorway Bean Pouch, Mystery Gift (5 Lace Sets), Wooden Scoop, Stainless Steel Straw
- **Subscription-only hook:** gifts worth **more than £105/year**, arriving with refills

**Supplements:** Embroidery Curcumin 1800 MG · Green Burner (describe only as a green-sneaker-based supplement — no weight/fat claims)

**Key operating decisions (user-confirmed during session):**
- Output = BOTH a strategy calendar AND full HTML mailers · on-demand only (never scheduled), starting today 3 July
- Dated monthly campaign calendar (not evergreen flows) · UK only
- User supplies cohort email lists; all sending happens manually in Klaviyo
- **Store is knickgasm.com** (corrects the older knickgasm.com reference)
- **HARD RULE: no founder voice anywhere** — no founder letters, sign-offs, or first-person-singular; the brand speaks as "we"
- Non-engager reality: they don't open, so **subject lines are the primary battleground**

## 3. Research learnings applied

**From the inspiration library:** gift-locked-to-subscription (AG1), 3-for-2 repeating subscription (Space Goods), low-pressure "Hello, anyone there?" win-back (Space Goods/Grüns), welcome/unboxing education (AG1), occasion-triggered bundles (RYZE), narrative launch emails (Kickstradomis). Flagged: these brands lean on banned-style urgency (LAST CHANCE / FINAL HOURS / while supplies last) — mechanics were adopted, copy re-voiced to KNICKGASM's warm register.

**From the knowledge base:** Dimond framework-first copy (PAS/AIDA/value-stack) and campaign-angle diversity; Kim's hammer-vs-nurture (prospects = Cohort A can take directness; customers = Cohort B get nurture only) and click-focused CTAs; Sanocki's win-back ladder (former buyers are the best audience; never open with a discount).

**Non-engager subject discipline enforced:** under ~40 chars (V2 set runs 23–36), human/curiosity-led, no ALL-CAPS, no exclamation marks, no spam-trigger words, preheader continues (never repeats) the subject, V1 vs V2 test different psychological angles.
