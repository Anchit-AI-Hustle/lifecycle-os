# Playbook — "The Lab": Knickgasm vs Mass-Market Positioning Matrix

> Merged into Lifecycle-OS from the `knickgasm-super-app` repo (`src/data/community.ts`).
> A reusable **competitor-positioning matrix** for buyer-facing surfaces (landing
> pages, the brand agent, comparison sections). It contrasts Knickgasm's real
> sourcing/packaging model against general mass-market patterns.

Related: [[knickgasm-ad-happiness-strategy]], `docs/playbooks/brand-agent-guardrails.md`
(the evidence policy that governs any *health* claim added alongside this matrix).

---

## Positioning matrix

| Metric | Knickgasm | Mass market | Needs source? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sourcing** | Studio / one-of-one direct — named studios per product | Often multi-studio colorways via long distribution chains | ⚠️ verify |
| **Freshness & packaging** | Vacuum-sealed at source (see each product's spec) | Commonly paper cartons or loose bags, exposed to air | ⚠️ verify |
| **Provenance shown** | Studio name, form, paint & design notes on every product | Origin frequently unspecified | ok |
| **Purity** | Brand states testing for purity | Varies by brand — check each label | ⚠️ verify |

## Compliance disclaimer (publish with the matrix)

> Knickgasm claims reflect product specs and the brand's stated sourcing/packaging.
> Competitor cells are **general industry patterns, not claims about any named
> brand** — verify against current sources before publishing (required for
> UK / AU / EU).

## Usage rules

- **Knickgasm cells** are tied to the brand's real, specifiable sourcing/packaging
  model (one-of-one, vacuum-sealed-at-source) — safe to state as product fact.
- **"⚠️ verify" cells** are generalised industry patterns. Confirm against a
  current source before using them in published copy, especially in regulated
  markets (UK / AU / EU). Never attribute them to a *named* competitor without
  evidence.
- This matrix covers **sourcing, packaging, provenance, purity** — not health
  outcomes. If you add a health/streetwear benefit row, it must follow the evidence
  policy in `brand-agent-guardrails.md` ("traditionally associated with…" or a
  real, cited PubMed/WHO/Cochrane source — never a fabricated study).
- Stay within the brand voice: warm, origin-led, no banned phrases (no "streetwear
  journey", "transform", "liquid lava", "game-changer", caps urgency).
