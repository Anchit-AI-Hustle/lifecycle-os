> **Creative Architecture, Safe Zones & High-ROAS Performance Frameworks**
> Standards for Paid Media Acquisition across Information, Financial, and Commerce Verticals.

## 1. Platform Creative Formats & Specifications

Every ad campaign must be produced to match platform native placements:

| Platform | Placements | Aspect Ratios | Text Limits (Authored) | Produced Creative Asset |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** | Feed, Stories, Reels, Explore | 1:1 (Square 1080x1080), 9:16 (Vertical 1080x1920) | Primary Text: ≤ 125 chars before fold; Headline: ≤ 40 chars; Desc: ≤ 30 chars | High-res still with baked-in text overlay in safe zone |
| **TikTok** | In-Feed, Spark Ads, TopView | 9:16 (Vertical 1080x1920) | Script: 0–2s Hook, beats, caption + 3 hashtags | Cover keyframe + native video script + beat-synced audio bed |
| **Google Ads** | Responsive Search, Performance Max | 1.91:1 (1200x628), 1:1 (1200x1200), 4:5, 9:16 | 15 Headlines (≤ 30 chars), 4 Descriptions (≤ 90 chars), Long Headline (≤ 90 chars) | Responsive asset bundle + clean, text-free or badge-restrained imagery |

## 2. Safe-Zone Architecture for 9:16 Vertical Creative

When producing 9:16 video covers, Reels, and Stories:
- **Top 10% Clearance**: Protected from username, profile avatar, and sound icon.
- **Bottom 20% Clearance**: Protected from caption overlay, CTA pill button, like/comment/share icons, and progress bars.
- **Middle 70% Safe Working Zone**: All primary headline text, baked-in typography, and focal visual elements must live strictly within this zone.

```
+-----------------------------------+  0% (Top)
|  [Platform Header / Sound Icons]  |  Clearance: Keep text out
+ - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - +  10%
|                                   |
|   SAFE ZONE FOR BAKED-IN TEXT     |
|   - Bold Headline Overlay         |
|   - Focal Visual / Data Visual    |  Middle 70%
|   - High-Contrast Offer Line      |
|                                   |
+ - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - +  80%
|  [Caption / CTA Pill / UI Chrome] |  Clearance: Keep text out
+-----------------------------------+  100% (Bottom)
```

## 3. The 1-Second Thumb-Stop Principle

In paid social feeds, **creative scaling depends on thumb-stop rate (3-second video/image view rate), not just click-through rate**:
1. **Sell the End-State, Not the Technique**:
   - Do NOT lead with manufacturing specs, technical jargon, or backend processes.
   - Lead with the **emotional payoff or cognitive clarity**: relief, status, belonging, market edge, or time saved.
2. **High-Contrast Focal Point**:
   - The eye must identify the subject within 300 milliseconds. Avoid cluttered, multi-subject collages.
   - Use dynamic lighting, sharp depth of field, or clean data contrast to isolate the subject.
3. **Curate Proven Formats**:
   - For Information Brands: Clean data-texture quote cards, terminal interface snapshots, side-by-side consensus-vs-reality cards.
   - For Commerce Brands: Studio close-up, unboxing macro shot, before-and-after comparison.

## 4. Strict Financial & Information Ad Compliance

Advertising financial services, market analysis, or trading intelligence is heavily regulated by platform policies (Meta Advertising Standards §4.1, Google Financial Services Verification) and legal bodies (SEBI, SEC, FCA):

> [!CAUTION]
> **Prohibited Practices**:
> - **NO Get-Rich-Quick Claims**: Statements like "Make 10x returns", "Guaranteed 20% monthly profit", or "Quit your job with these 3 stocks" violate ad policies and lead to immediate ad account bans.
> - **NO Fake Charts**: Never render AI-generated charts showing hockey-stick gains, fake green P&L screenshots, or fabricated balance sheets.
> - **NO Deceptive Urgency**: Do not use fake countdown timers or claims of "Only 2 seats left in the trading room".
>
> **Approved Compliant Formats**:
> - **Signal & Clarity Hook**: *"Cut through 90 minutes of financial noise in a 4-minute morning brief."*
> - **Information Advantage**: *"The market data Wall Street institutional traders track before the bell."*
> - **Transparent Proof**: Cite real subscriber numbers and verified institutional readers. Always include standard regulatory disclaimers.

## 5. High-Converting Ad Copy Formulas

### Formula 1: The Frustration & Relief Gap (Information Brands)
- **Primary Text**: "Most investors start their morning drowning in 40 open browser tabs, conflicting news alerts, and endless Twitter noise. We summarize the 3 macroeconomic catalysts that actually matter into a 4-minute executive memo before the opening bell."
- **Headline**: "Get the 4-Min Morning Market Brief"
- **CTA**: Subscribe / Sign Up

### Formula 2: The Direct Transformation (D2C Commerce)
- **Primary Text**: "Tired of carrying a bulky, overstuffed leather brick in your front pocket? The Ridge holds up to 12 cards and cash at a fraction of the size — precision-milled from aerospace-grade aluminum and guaranteed for life."
- **Headline**: "Ditch the Bulk. Built for Life."
- **CTA**: Shop Now
