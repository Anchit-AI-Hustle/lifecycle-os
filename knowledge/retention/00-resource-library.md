## 6. Books (the durable theory layer)

- ◉ **The Loyalty Effect** and **Winning on Purpose** — Fred Reichheld (Bain). The academic root of retention economics and NPS; source of the canonical "5% retention improvement → 25–95% profit increase" research.
- ◉ **Customer Centricity** (and **The Customer-Base Audit**) — Peter Fader. CLV thinking, whale-focused strategy — the rigorous version of Sanocki's whale/minnow framework.
- ◉ **Never Lose a Customer Again** — Joey Coleman. The first-100-days framework for post-purchase experience.
- ◉ **Hooked** — Nir Eyal. Habit loops; the psychology behind repeat engagement.
- ◉ **The Membership Economy** and **The Forever Transaction** — Robbie Kellman Baxter. Subscription/membership strategy.
- ◉ **Subscribed** — Tien Tzuo (Zuora). The subscription business model end-to-end.
- ◉ **Retention Point** — Robert Skrob. Short, tactical membership-retention playbook.
- ◉ **Influence** — Robert Cialdini. Persuasion principles every retention copywriter borrows.
- ◉ **Hacking Growth** — Sean Ellis & Morgan Brown. Includes the retention/engagement chapters that shaped SaaS-style retention testing.

## 10. A suggested learning path

1. **Foundations (week 1–2):** Klaviyo Academy's Digital Marketing certificate (esp. "Retain customers long-term") + Reichheld's HBR classics → you now have the vocabulary and the math.
2. **Systems (week 3–4):** Jimmy Kim's interviews/SendIt back-catalog + Sanocki's Karmaloop case study and Nerd Marketing turnaround tips → the machine and the money.
3. **Execution (month 2):** Chase Dimond's newsletter archive + Joanne Coffey's published case studies (Klaviyo funnel guide, TYCA episode) + Really Good Emails/Milled swipe research → build your flows and calendar.
4. **Specialize (month 3+):** Perrelli for subscriptions, Weiss for CX/post-purchase, Murray for creative velocity, CXL or Magnet Monster if you want a paid deep-dive; join one community (eCom Email Certified) for feedback loops.

## 11. A note on context (non-US application)

Almost everything above is written from a US/Shopify/Klaviyo-centric vantage point. The frameworks (RFM, churn curves, zero-party data, flow architecture, the 20–30% owned-revenue benchmark) transfer globally, but channel mix does not: in markets like India, WhatsApp Business API frequently outperforms both email and SMS as the primary retention surface, COD reconfirmation/RTO-reduction flows are retention-critical in ways US content never covers, and ESP economics differ. Treat these resources as the strategy layer and adapt the channel layer locally.
