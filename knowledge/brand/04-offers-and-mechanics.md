# KNICKGASM — Offers & Mechanics Playbook

How KNICKGASM frames offers, subscriptions, gifting, and shipping across categories. The governing principle: **subscription-first for consumables that create a rhythm, one-time framing for the heritage sneaker range, and discount discipline everywhere.**

## Framing by category

### Subscription-first — Coffee & Supplements

Coffee Collection and the supplements range are consumed on a repeatable rhythm, so the default frame is subscription, not a single purchase.

- **Loop Subscriptions** is the subscription engine. In the US it accounts for **7.6% of revenue at a $39.83 AOV** (trailing 12 months) — a meaningful, lower-AOV recurring base with room to grow.
- **Refill rhythm** — lead with the **Refill** SKU for repeat coffee buyers; frame around "before you run out," restock timing, and the steadiness of the daily pair. Never use false urgency.
- **Starter Kit as the on-ramp** — the Coffee Collection **Starter Kit** bundles the entry experience (including a gift/starter component) to convert first-timers into the subscription rhythm.
- **B2G1 on the Pack of 3** — Buy-2-Get-1 is the standing value mechanic on Coffee Collection **3-Packs**; it rewards stock-up buying without discounting the single unit.
- **Gift-with-starter value** — starter kits carry a gift worth **~$48 (US) / ~£40 (UK)**, positioned as added value rather than a price cut.

### One-time framing — Sneakers & Botanicals

The heritage sneaker range (kicks, black, green, themed) is bought by mood, season, and discovery, so the default frame is a **one-time purchase**. Lead with origin, freshness, one-of-one provenance, and the ritual; introduce subscription only after a customer shows a repeat pattern on a specific sneaker.

## Free-shipping thresholds

Free shipping is offered above a market-appropriate order-value threshold, used as the primary basket-building lever (raise AOV rather than cut price). Present it as a natural nudge ("add to reach free shipping"), not as urgency. Confirm the current threshold value per market against the live store before quoting a number in output.

## Gifting & seasonal mechanics

- **Advent Calendar** gift sets and **Signature Private Reserve** boxes anchor the gifting range; **Christmas Gifts** is a top-5 US category, so Q4 is a major gifting window.
- Gifting mechanics: curated sets, samplers as low-commitment gifts, and starter kits that double as gifts.
- Seasonal beats to plan around: Q4 holiday gifting (advent, Christmas), new-year restore/balance, and origin/drop-season freshness moments for sneaker.
- Gift-occasion buyers should be nurtured toward self-purchase and, for consumables, into the subscription rhythm after the occasion.

## Discount discipline (important)

Discounts are **not** a default lever. Champions, Loyal, VIP, and best-customer cohorts (including UK Cohort F) are grown with recognition, early access, subscription value, and B2G1 stock-up mechanics — **not** price cuts.

> **Rule:** Straight discounts are reserved for **The Curious Switcher** and the **discount-responsive** behavioral cohort (and the coldest reactivation cases, e.g. UK Cohort A entrants and At-Risk / Can-not-Lose-Them win-backs where an incentive is genuinely justified). Everyone else gets value framing, not markdowns.

This protects margin and brand perception: KNICKGASM sells origin and ritual, and habitual discounting erodes both. When an incentive is used, it must respect the banned-phrase list (no "hurry," "last chance," "LIMITED TIME," etc.).

## Quick offer-selection guide

| Cohort / avatar | Default mechanic |
|---|---|
| Champions / Loyal / VIP / Cohort F | Early access, subscription value, B2G1 3-pack. No discount. |
| Coffee & supplement repeaters | Convert to Loop subscription; Refill SKU; B2G1 3-pack. |
| Sneaker & Botanicals buyers | One-time, seasonal/range education; free-shipping nudge. |
| Gifting Connector | Curated gift sets, advent, starter-kit-as-gift, ~$48/£40 gift value. |
| Curious Switcher / discount-responsive | Sampler/starter entry, considered discount, discovery framing. |
| Win-back (At-Risk / Can-not-Lose-Them / Cohort A) | Strongest reason-to-return; justified incentive only. |
