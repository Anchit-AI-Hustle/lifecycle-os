Design system

Every surface of the app, rendered live in the active brand. The platform's own mark stays the platform's; every colour below is a role from the surface contract (design/lifecycle-os/CONTRACT.md), derived from the brand's record so it stays readable for any palette.

Platform identity

The tab icon, the touch icon, the manifest, the share card and the rail mark identify the TOOL, so they are Lifecycle OS's mark and never a tenant's. The mark's three colours are neutral greys by the page audit's own saturation rule. The active brand's logo appears only in the brand slot beneath the wordmark.

Inverse, on a brand band
The glyph as a mask, filled with --vh-on-band: the band is sectionGround(), the colour on it is textOn().

Colour roles

The brand layer, as tokens() derived it for the brand on screen now. A fill is never text: brand colour as text is a *-text token, text on a fill is its on-* token. Values are read from the page as it renders.

Elevation
lift 1lift 2

Two lifts, both neutral shadows. Lift 1 for anything in the flow; lift 2 only for what floats over the page, with room below it, because its shadow falls on the text underneath. Depth, not darkness, carries the instrument look.

Motion

Spring easing for lift and glow, a soft ease for fades and the skeleton shimmer, a one-time reveal on scroll. Reduced motion means less motion, never less content: every animation stops and nothing that was hidden for it stays hidden.

Live indicator: the dot is visible without the pulse.
