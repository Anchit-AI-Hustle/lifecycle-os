Inside the studio

How a blank Air Force 1 becomes a hand-painted anime grail

No prints. No transfers. Every KNICKGASM custom is painted by hand, panel by panel, by India's best sneaker artists. Here is the full process.

Phase 01

The canvas: a 100% original sneaker

Every build starts with a brand-new original pair, Nike Air Force 1, Jordan 1, Dunks, Court Vision or Converse. We never touch replicas. The leather is prepped and cleaned so the paint bonds properly.

Why it matters: original leather takes professional sneaker paint cleanly, which is what keeps fine linework crisp for years.
Phase 02

The sketch

The artist studies the source, Tanjiro's haori pattern, Gojo's blindfold, the exact orange of Goku's gi, then maps the composition across toe box, swoosh, quarter panels and heel so the design reads from every angle.

Character accuracy is the whole job. Fans notice everything, and our artists are fans first.
Phase 03

The paint

Layers of professional-grade sneaker paint go down one at a time: base blocks, mid tones, shading, then the fine detail passes for eyes, linework and effects. A single pair takes hours of brush time.

This is why each pair is one-of-one. Two artists painting the same brief will never produce identical strokes.
Phase 04

The seal

A protective finish locks the artwork in, making it water and scratch resistant. These customs are made to be worn on the street, in the rain, at the con, not kept in a box.
