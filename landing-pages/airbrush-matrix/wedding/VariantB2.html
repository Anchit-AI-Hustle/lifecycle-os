The Craft

What actually happens between "here is my outfit" and a finished pair

A KNICKGASM wedding custom is not a print job. It is days of hand-painting by some of India's best sneaker artists. Here is the process, stage by stage.

01

The canvas: an original, always

Every custom starts with a brand-new, 100% original sneaker: Nike Air Force 1, Jordan, Dunk, Converse, or Adidas. We buy the authentic pair in your size before a single drop of paint touches it. The factory finish is then prepped and stripped where the artwork will sit, so paint bonds to the leather instead of sitting on top of it.

02

The reference: your outfit

You share photos of your wedding outfit. The artist studies the actual embroidery, the exact shade of the fabric, the motifs in the dupatta or the pocket square, and sketches a composition that belongs to that outfit and no other.

This is why the Wedding Carnival pair is listed as "customisable with the outfit": the listing is the starting point, your outfit is the brief.
03

The paint: layers, by hand

Colours are mixed and applied in thin layers, built up over days. Fine linework, shading, and lettering are done with brushes, not stencils. Crystal and bling work, when the design calls for it, is set stone by stone.

04

The seal: made for the dance floor

Finished artwork is sealed with a water and scratch resistant finish. Your pair is made to be worn: through the baraat, the sangeet, the after-party, and every anniversary after.
