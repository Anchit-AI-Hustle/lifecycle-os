Inside the studio

What it actually takes to ice out an Air Force 1

A crystal custom is not decorated, it is crafted. Here is the full process, from a boxed original Nike to a sealed one-of-one, stage by stage.

Stage 01

Source the original

Every build starts with a brand-new, 100% original Nike Air Force 1. No blanks, no replicas, ever. The authentic sneaker is the canvas.

Stage 02

Sketch and map

The artist sketches the design onto your exact size and silhouette, then maps stone placement panel by panel so the crystal work follows the shoe's lines instead of fighting them.

Stage 03

Hand-paint the base

Base coats and colorway details go on by brush. This is the same hand-painting craft behind every KNICKGASM custom, done by India's best artists.

Stage 04

Set the stones

Crystals and studs are set one at a time. A swoosh alone is a field of individual placements, which is why a pair like the Sapphire Dragon reads like jewellery up close.

Stage 05

Seal and quality-check

The finished pair is sealed with a water and scratch resistant finish, checked, and boxed. Made to order, typically delivered in 10-15 days.

The craft standard, in plain terms

  • 100% original brand sneakers, always
  • Hand-painted and hand-set, no printed transfers
  • Water and scratch resistant sealed designs
  • One-of-one: your pair is not repeated
  • Express shipping worldwide, 60+ countries
