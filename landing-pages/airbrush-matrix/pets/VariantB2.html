Inside the studio

What It Actually Takes to Hand-Paint Your Dog on an Air Force 1

No prints. No heat transfers. No shortcuts. Here's the craft that goes into every one-of-one pair in the KNICKGASM Pets collection.

Stage 01

The canvas: 100% originals only

Every build starts with an original pair — Nike Air Force 1, Adidas Sambas. We buy the real shoe because leather quality decides how paint sits, flexes and lasts. Replicas never enter the studio.

Stage 02

The sketch: your pet, studied

The artist works from your photo — the exact fur pattern, the one ear that never sits right, the eyes. The portrait is sketched straight onto the leather panels so it flows with the shape of the shoe instead of fighting it.

Stage 03

The paint: layer over layer

Premium leather paints, thin coats, built up patiently. Fur is the hardest subject in sneaker art — it takes fine brushwork to make a golden retriever's coat look soft on a hard leather toe box. This is why every pair is made to order.

Stage 04

The seal: made for the street

A water & scratch resistant finish locks the portrait in. These are wearers, not shelf pieces — rain, scuffs and daily rotation are part of the plan.
