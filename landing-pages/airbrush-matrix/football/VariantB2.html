The Craft

A club crest is unforgiving. That’s the point.

Fans notice everything — a shade of red that’s off, a badge detail missing. So every KNICKGASM football custom is built the slow way: by hand, on an original sneaker, by artists who treat the crest like scripture.

The build, stage by stage

Stage 1 — The canvas

We start with a 100% original brand sneaker — Air Force 1, Court Vision or Sambas straight from the box. Panels are prepped so paint bonds to the leather instead of sitting on top of it.

Stage 2 — The sketch

The artist plots the crest, club colours and supporting details across toe box, swoosh and heel. Composition is decided before a single drop of paint touches leather.

Stage 3 — The paint

Freehand layers, thin coat over thin coat, so the finish flexes with the shoe instead of cracking. Crest linework goes on last, when the base colours have set.

Stage 4 — The seal

A finishing coat makes the artwork water & scratch resistant. These are made to be worn — matchday, airport, everywhere.
