The build, start to finish

Canvas

An original pair, sourced fresh

Nike Air Force 1, Jordan, Dunk, Court Vision, Converse or Adidas — always 100% original brand sneakers. The canvas matters as much as the paint.

Sketch

The design is mapped to the shoe

Artists sketch the composition around the sneaker's own lines — panels, stitching, swoosh — so the final piece looks born on the shoe, not stuck on it.

Prep

Factory finish comes off

Panels are cleaned and prepped so paint bonds to the leather itself. This is the unglamorous step that separates customs that last from customs that crack.

Paint

Layer by layer, by hand

India's best custom artists build the design in thin layers — base tones, detail work, line art, highlights. No prints. No transfers. Just brushes and patience.

Seal

Locked in for real wear

A water & scratch resistant finish seals every painted panel. The pair is made to be worn in the real world — rain, streets, dance floors.

Ship

Boxed and sent worldwide

Made to order in 10–15 days, then express shipped to 60+ countries. Straight from the studio to their doorstep.
