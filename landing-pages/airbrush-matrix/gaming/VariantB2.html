This is exactly what happens between your order and your doorstep — the full KNICKGASM process behind every game and superhero custom.

Stage 01The canvas

Start with the real thing

Every custom begins as a 100% original brand sneaker — Nike Air Force 1, Jordan, Dunk, Court Vision, Converse or Adidas. No replicas, ever. The leather panels of an AF1 are the best canvas in footwear, which is why artists love them.

Stage 02The sketch

Composition before colour

The artist breaks your design down across toe box, quarter panels, swoosh and heel — so a Valorant agent or a Joker grin reads clean from every angle, not just head-on.

  • Character placement mapped panel by panel
  • Colorway locked to the game or hero's palette
  • Design approved before a drop of paint touches leather
Stage 03The paint

Brush, airbrush, repeat

Layers of flexible leather paint go down by hand — base coats, blocking, shading, line work, highlights. Fine detail like eyes, logos and texture is the slowest part, and the reason no two pairs can ever be identical.

Stage 04The seal

Built for the streets

A protective finish locks the artwork in, making it water & scratch resistant. Then the pair is quality-checked, boxed and express-shipped — we deliver to 60+ countries, with a made-to-order window of 10-15 days.
