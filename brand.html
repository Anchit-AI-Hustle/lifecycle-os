Brand Input

The single brand-truth record for this platform. Every generator - mailers, ads, landing pages, the Smart Brain and KicksGPT - reads these values, so changing them here changes every asset the OS produces next. This is also what you edit first when standing the platform up for a new brand.

How this is wired: saved to the lifecycle_brand_kit singleton in Supabase via /api/kb?action=brand-kit. The palette and typography here must stay in step with api/_shared/master-prompt.js (the prompt-side brand block) - the Verify panel below flags drift.
