Who holds what

The important question for any connection of this kind is what the platform ends up holding, and what stays with the merchant.

PCI scope

This platform renders no card field anywhere, so card data never reaches it. That keeps the platform out of card-data scope, and it does not remove the merchant's own obligations on their own checkout.

Webhooks

Without these, a stored connection quietly rots: a merchant who disconnects at the provider would still read as connected here.

What a human has to do once

An operator cannot self-serve any of this. Each gateway needs the platform owner to register with the provider first, and the resulting identifiers go into environment variables, never into the code.
