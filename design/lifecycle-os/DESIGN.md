## Overview

**Lifecycle OS** is a universal lifecycle-marketing platform: any brand onboards, and the whole app
runs as that brand, in its palette, its type, its name and its copy. So this design system has two
layers, and keeping them apart is the point of it.

- **The platform layer** is Lifecycle OS's own and never a tenant's: the mark, the logo set, the
  wordmark, the tab icon, the touch and app icons, the share card, the icon set and the
  illustrations. Its colours are the three neutral greys of the mark (`colors` above), neutral by
  the page audit's own saturation rule (`scripts/audit-pages.js` `lowSat`).
- **The brand layer** is every colour a person sees on a screen. No page chooses a colour: it
  chooses a ROLE (page ground, panel, sunken panel, brand band, text, link, focus ring...) from
  the surface contract (`design/lifecycle-os/CONTRACT.md`), and the role's value is derived from
  the active brand's record by `tokens()` (`api/_shared/brand-workspace-core.js`), painted by
  `brand-context.js`. The derivation is what keeps any palette readable.

_The front matter above is generated by `scripts/build-design-system.js` from the files that hold
each value (the mark SVG, `theme.css`, `tokens()`); the prose is written by hand. Values the
platform does not own (a brand's colours, a brand's typefaces) are not tokens here: they belong to
that brand's own DESIGN.md, which the brand context pack emits from its site._

### Three kinds of mark

| Kind | Where | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Platform chrome | tab icon, touch icon, manifest, share card, the rail's wordmark and mark, the Android launcher | `assets/lifecycle-os-mark.svg` and the rasters `scripts/build-platform-mark.js` renders from it |
| The active brand's logo | the brand slot under the wordmark, the onboarding review | that brand's own record (`logo_url`), else a monogram of its name |
| Tenant zero's own artefacts | the frozen `diff-version` snapshot only | `favicon.png` |

### The mark and the logo set

The mark is a closed loop with an advancing arrowhead (the lifecycle that keeps running) around a
still core (the OS), on a rounded tile. Every logo file is a string transform of that one SVG plus
the wordmark, written by `node scripts/build-platform-mark.js --logos`, so no lockup can drift from
the tab icon.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `assets/lifecycle-os-mark.svg` | The mark: favicon, app icon, rail mark (inline as `LOGO_SVG` in `auth.js`, glyph in `currentColor`). |
| `assets/lifecycle-os/logos/lockup-horizontal.svg` (+ `.png` 1296x256) | Mark and wordmark on one line: page headers, documents. |
| `assets/lifecycle-os/logos/lockup-stacked.svg` (+ `.png` 480x336) | Mark above the wordmark: square placements, the about page. |
| `assets/lifecycle-os/logos/wordmark.svg` (+ `.png` 992x192) | The name alone, where the mark already appears nearby. |
| `assets/lifecycle-os/logos/mark-mono.svg` (+ `mark-mono-512.png`) | One ink, no tile fill: single-colour reproduction. |
| `assets/lifecycle-os/logos/mark-glyph.svg` | The loop alone in `currentColor`: the inverse mark on a brand band (`.los-mark-on-band`, a CSS mask filled with `--vh-on-band`). |
| `assets/lifecycle-os-32.png`, `-180`, `-192`, `-512`, `-512-maskable`, `-og.png` | Favicon fallback, touch icon, manifest icons, maskable icon, share card (unchanged paths). |

- **Clear space:** a quarter of the mark's height on every side (16 units of its 64-unit tile). In
  a lockup, the wordmark sits 16 units from the tile.
- **Minimum sizes:** the mark 16px (favicon), the horizontal lockup 120px wide, the stacked lockup
  72px wide, the wordmark 96px wide.
- **The wordmark** is "Lifecycle OS" in the system sans at 700 (`--los-font-wordmark`), with a
  fixed `textLength` so it measures the same on every machine. It is never set in a tenant's face.
- **Inverse:** on a brand-coloured band the mark is the glyph alone, filled with `--vh-on-band`
  (the band is `sectionGround()`, the colour on it `textOn()`). The mark's own files stay neutral.
- **Misuse:** never recolour the mark in a brand's colours; never put the active brand's logo in
  the tab, the touch icon or the rail mark's place; never put the mark on a photograph or a dark
  neutral; never stretch, rotate, outline or add effects; never redraw the loop by hand (edit the
  SVG and re-run the script).

## Colors

The platform's own colours are the mark's three neutrals in the front matter: ink `#24292E`, tile
`#F5F6F8`, line `#D4D8DE`, with white as the surface they sit on. They are the only colours
Lifecycle OS owns. Every other colour on a screen is a ROLE, resolved per brand:

| Role | Token | Derived from the brand by |
|---|---|---|
| Page ground, rail | `--vh-bg` | `palette.surface` (validated light) |
| Panel, card, modal | `--vh-panel` | `palette.surface_alt` |
| Sunken panel (status, failure, notice) | `--vh-panel-2` | `sunkenSurface()`: darkened only while every text token keeps 4.5:1 |
| Brand band (a section in the brand's colour) | `--vh-band` / `--vh-on-band` | `sectionGround(primary, accent, surface)` / `textOn(band)` |
| Body text, headings, icons | `--vh-ink` / `--vh-heading` | `palette.ink` |
| Secondary text | `--vh-ink-dim` | `readableAsText(muted, worst surface, 4.9)` |
| Brand colour as text, links | `--vh-primary-text`, `--vh-link`, `--vh-accent-text` | `readableAsText(colour, worst surface, 4.9)` |
| Primary control fill and its label | `--vh-primary` / `--vh-on-primary` | `palette.primary` / `readableOn(...)`, held to 4.5:1 at activation |
| State words and dots | `--vh-ok-text`, `--vh-warn-text`, `--vh-err-text` | `readableAsText(state, worst surface, 4.9)` |
| State edges and fills | `--vh-ok`, `--vh-warn`, `--vh-err` | `palette.ok/warn/err` (never text) |
| Lines | `--vh-line`, `--vh-line-hot` | `shade(ink, .84)`, `shade(ink, .68)` |
| Focus ring | `--vh-focus` | `readableAsText(accent, worst surface, 3)` |

The rules (each one enforced by `tests/design-system.spec.js`):

1. **No section ground is a dark neutral** (luminance under 0.12 and saturation under 0.25). A
   brand band goes through `sectionGround()`, so a near-black primary falls to the accent, then
   the surface. A control may be dark; a section may not.
2. **Every text/ground pair meets AA for any palette** the brand layer accepts: brand-coloured text
   only through a `*-text` token and only on the grounds it was adjusted for; text on a fill only
   through that fill's `on-*` token.
3. **No colour literal in a component.** The only literals are the mark's neutrals (an SVG favicon
   cannot read the page) and the generated no-brand fallbacks.
4. **Light only, by design.** `validatePalette()` refuses a dark-neutral surface, so there is no
   dark theme; depth and motion carry the instrument look instead.

`design/lifecycle-os/CONTRACT.md` has the full surface table and the derived values for six test
palettes (the platform neutral, the example brand (tenant zero), a red primary, a pale primary, a
near-black primary and a bare record).

## Typography

Three families, two of them the brand's:

- **Headings** `--vh-font-head` = the active brand's heading stack (`--brand-font-head`).
- **Text** `--vh-font-body` = the brand's body stack.
- **Metadata and code** `--vh-font-mono`: eyebrows, column heads, chips, identifiers, with wide
  tracking and tabular figures. Never body copy.
- **The wordmark** `--los-font-wordmark`: the system sans, the platform's only face.

The scale (the front matter's sizes are each step's upper bound; in the app the larger steps are
fluid, `clamp()` between a phone and a desktop):

## Layout

- **Spacing** is the seven-step scale in the front matter (`--vh-s1` 4px to `--vh-s7` 48px):
  4 and 8 inside controls, 12 between related rows, 16 card padding and grid gaps, 22 panel
  padding, 32 between sections, 48 between page regions.
- **The shell:** a fixed 248px rail on the left at 961px and wider; under 960px the rail is a
  drawer behind the menu button, under a 50px bar in the brand's surface. Content max width
  1180px (`.vh-container`), 16px side gutters on a phone, no horizontal scroll.
- **Grids** auto-fill with a 280px minimum column (`.vh-grid`), one column on a phone. Parallel
  cards are equal size and aligned.
- **Touch:** 44px minimum tap targets for the shell's controls at phone width; safe-area insets
  for the notch and the home indicator.
- **Tables:** header cells sit over their columns (`table-layout: fixed`), the first column left,
  the rest centred, tabular figures, wide tables scroll inside their own wrapper.

## Elevation & Depth

The futuristic character comes from depth, motion and precision on the brand's own light
surface, never from turning the lights off:

- **Ambient field:** two fixed layers behind everything, an aurora drawn from the active brand's
  primary and accent at low opacity and a fine technical grid masked out of the middle.
- **Frosted surfaces:** cards are `--vh-glass` (white at .72) with a backdrop blur (always with its
  `-webkit-` pair, behind `@supports`), and a 2px energy line in the brand's primary-to-accent
  along the top edge. The rail is never frosted: its text tokens are tuned against the brand
  surface.
- **Two lifts:** `--vh-lift-1` for anything in the flow, `--vh-lift-2` only for what floats over
  the page. A lift-2 shadow falling on text below it took muted copy under AA in the rendered
  gate, so inline components take lift-1.
- **Scrim:** a modal dims the page with `--vh-ink` at .45 opacity. It is not a section.
- **Layers:** rail 100, notice bar 120, modal 1000, toast 1100.
- **Motion:** a spring (`--vh-ease-spring`) for lift and glow, a soft ease for fades and the
  skeleton shimmer, a one-time reveal on scroll (`motion.js`). **Reduced motion means less
  motion, never less content:** every animation and transition drops to near zero, and nothing
  hidden for an animation stays hidden (iOS turns reduced motion on in Low Power Mode too).

## Shapes

- **Radius:** `--vh-r-sm` 8px (controls, inputs, chips' inner shapes), `--vh-r-md` 12px (toasts,
  swatches), `--vh-r-lg` 16px (cards, panels, modals), `--vh-r-pill` for chips, badges, the credit
  pill. A brand whose own site measures a control or card radius overrides sm and lg through
  `--brand-radius-control` and `--brand-radius-card`.
- **The mark's tile** is rounded at 15 of 64 units; the maskable and touch icons fill the square
  because their launchers crop it themselves.
- **Icons:** one stroke set on a 24px grid, 1.75 stroke, round caps and joins, `currentColor`
  (`assets/lifecycle-os/icons.svg`, 62 symbols, `design/lifecycle-os/icons.json` lists the emoji
  each replaces). Third-party marks (the ad platforms) stay in their owners' colours and are not
  part of this set.
- **Illustrations:** six empty and placeholder states on a 128x96 grid, drawn only in `--vh-ill-*`
  tokens (`assets/lifecycle-os/illustrations.svg`), so they take the active brand's colours.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do pick a role token for every colour; don't write a hex, an `rgb()` or a named colour in a
  component.
- Do paint a brand-coloured section with `.vh-band`; don't put `var(--brand-primary)` on a
  section background (a near-black brand would get a black section).
- Do write brand-coloured text with a `*-text` token on the page, a panel or the sunken panel;
  don't write `color: var(--brand-primary)`, and don't put brand-coloured text on a tint or a band.
- Do derive text on a fill (`--vh-on-primary`, `--vh-on-band`); don't assume white.
- Do keep the mark, the wordmark and the tab icon Lifecycle OS's; don't put a tenant's logo or
  colour in them, and don't put another tenant's file in the brand slot.
- Do show a gap as a `[DATA REQUIRED BEFORE LAUNCH: ...]` marker; don't fill it with a plausible
  value, a placeholder photo or another brand's fact.
- Do say an ordinary state (signed out, on this device, not connected) in the accent rule; don't
  dress it as a failure or a warning.
- Do use the stroke icon set; don't use emoji as UI icons.
- Do keep inline components on lift-1; don't let a lift-2 shadow fall on text.
- Don't design a dark theme: the system is light-only by design.
