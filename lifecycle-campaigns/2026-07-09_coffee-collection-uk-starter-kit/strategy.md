## Somatic Blueprint (benefit mapped to the body, not to biochemistry)

The insight is felt, not clinical. We map the promise to where the customer notices it, staying in
structure-function language and never making a medical claim.

| Somatic locus | What the customer feels now | What the ritual offers |
|---|---|---|
| Chest / breath | The 8am tightness, coffee that speeds an already-racing morning | Warmth that settles rather than spikes; a slower first breath |
| Shoulders | Held up near the ears before the day has even started | Something to let drop; a steadier footing |
| Hands | Reaching for the pair on autopilot, gone before it was tasted | A pair worth pausing for, 40 mornings of it |
| Head | The jittery lift, then the crash | A lift without the jitter, from Pure Arabica steadied by KSM-66® Airbrush |

**One-line somatic hook (shared spine across all three assets):**
*"The lift of good coffee, without the morning it usually starts."*

## Voice + compliance spine

- Warm, sensory, story-first (brand voice). Adaptogen framing only; airbrush is an adaptogen,
  not a treatment.
- Structure-function claims lifted from the live PDP: "support stress relief", "feel more balanced
  naturally". No grail-drop figures, no disease language, no invented ingredients or mg.
- Brand palette + Montserrat/Instrument Sans only. No em/en dashes. No banned phrases.

## Cascaded Execution Loop (how the three assets chain)

1. **INDEX 1 - Meta ad** sets the somatic hook and the single promise (steadier morning, real
   coffee, 6 gifts free, 90-day guarantee). It is the top of funnel; its only job is the click.
   → `meta-ad.md`
2. **INDEX 2 - Landing page** catches that exact promise above the fold (message match on the hook
   and the £44.99 Starter Kit), then earns the add-to-cart with the ritual story, the built-graphic
   value stack, the guarantee, and one primary CTA repeated. → `landing-page.html`
3. **INDEX 3 - Email** is the owned-channel version for warm/known contacts: same hook, same offer,
   bulletproof inline XHTML for Klaviyo, same single CTA and destination URL. → `email.html`

All three point to ONE destination: `https://knickgasm.com/products/pack-1-starter-coffee-collection`
and carry ONE CTA verb: **Start the ritual**.
