## Positioning (art on your feet, never a drink)

Coffee here is a palette and a culture, not a drink. Every asset sells the artwork: deep-brown
and mocha tones hand-painted on 100% original Nike Air Force 1, Jordan 1 Lows, Court Vision
and Adidas Sambas. No drink language anywhere: nothing here is brewed, poured or consumed.

**One-line hook (shared spine across all three assets):**
*"Coffee tones, hand-painted on original Nikes."*

Supporting line for the culture angle: *"Everyone has white AF1s. Yours came out of an art studio."*

## Channels (Cascaded Execution Loop)

1. **INDEX 1 - Meta ad** (prospecting, UK, 18-40 sneaker + streetwear + cafe-culture interests)
   opens with the hook and the £89.09 entry point. Its only job is the click. → `meta-ad.md`
2. **INDEX 2 - Landing page** catches the identical hook above the fold (message match),
   runs real product photography, the craft claims, and a six-tile grid with PDP deep links.
   → `landing-page.html`
3. **INDEX 3 - Email** is the owned-channel launch for the UK list: same hook, four featured

All three point to ONE primary destination: `https://knickgasm.com/collections/coffee-air-forces`
and carry ONE CTA verb set: **Shop the Coffee Collection** (product cards may deep-link to PDPs).

## Voice + compliance spine

- Bold, energetic, street-culture, hype but authentic. Confident and playful, never corporate.
- Zero drink language (nothing brewed, poured, sipped or ordered). Coffee is art and colourway only.
- Only the six approved brand claims from `facts.md`. No invented discounts, reviews, or scarcity.
- Brand palette + Montserrat/Instrument Sans only. No em/en dashes. No banned phrases.
- White or red section backgrounds only, ink text on white, white text on red/ink buttons.
