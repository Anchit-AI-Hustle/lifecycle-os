- **No em/en dashes anywhere in output copy** - use commas, colons, or plain hyphens.
- **NO FOUNDER VOICE — HARD RULE**: no founder letters, no "from our founder/CEO", no personal-name sign-offs, no first-person-singular ("I") narration. The brand speaks as "we".
- **No health, wellness or medical language of any kind.** KNICKGASM sells artwork on footwear.
  Nothing here is ingested, and nothing here does anything to a body.
- **Voice**: bold, street-culture, hype but authentic; confident and playful, never corporate. Preferred words: custom, hand-painted, one-of-one, grail, canvas, colourway, drop, rotation, crafted, original. Exemplar sentence: "Everyone has white AF1s. Yours came out of an art studio."
