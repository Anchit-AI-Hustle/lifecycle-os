The Grail Drop

Every grail starts as a blank white pair on an artist's table.

This is the story of what happens next — inside the studio of India's largest sneaker customisers, where original Nike Air Force 1s become one-of-one hand-painted grails.

Shop the drop

The studio smells like acrylic and ambition.

A box of brand-new, 100% original Nike Air Force 1s arrives from the store shelf, not a back alley. An artist picks one up, turns it in the light, and starts sketching directly on the leather — a Saiyan aura curling over the swoosh, a club crest sized for the heel, a racing livery mapped panel by panel.

There is no print head anywhere in the building. Every line you see on a KNICKGASM pair was pulled by a human hand holding a brush. That is why no two pairs ever match, even when the design starts from the same idea.

One artist. One brush. One pair on the planet.

When the paint sets, the pair gets sealed so the design is water and scratch resistant — these grails are made to leave the house, not live in a display case. Then it ships express, from the studio in India to any of 60+ countries.
